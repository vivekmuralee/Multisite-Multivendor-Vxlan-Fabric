! MOTD banner - reference sample for manual config. Ansible equivalent:
!   roles/arista_base/templates/motd.j2, pushed with: ansible-playbook site.yml --tags banner
!
! "banner motd" is shown AFTER a successful login (SSH and console).
! "banner login" (optional, at the bottom) is shown BEFORE the password prompt; that is where
! an authorised-use warning belongs, since it is shown to everyone who connects.
!
! Syntax: everything between "banner motd" and a line containing only EOF is the banner,
! verbatim. Do not put "!" comment lines inside the block - they would become banner text.
! EOS banners have no hostname variable, so the per-switch lines below are filled in per
! device (example values are for arista-bl-lf-1).
!
banner motd
================================================================================
  arista-bl-lf-1  |  Border leaf  |  Multisite VXLAN EVPN fabric (lab)
  Mgmt: 192.168.122.26 (vrf mgmt)  |  ASN 65103  |  VTEP 10.255.1.13
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Logins are authenticated via TACACS+ (192.168.122.63), local as fallback.
  All commands are logged.
  Config is managed by Ansible (arista/site.yml) - avoid manual changes;
  use "configure session" + "commit timer" for any hand edits.
================================================================================
EOF
!
! Optional pre-login warning:
!
! banner login
! Authorized access only. Activity on this device is monitored and logged.
! EOF
!
! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Per-switch values for the second and third line:
!
!   arista-sp-1     Spine        Mgmt 192.168.122.24  ASN 65000  (no VTEP)
!   arista-sp-2     Spine        Mgmt 192.168.122.25  ASN 65000  (no VTEP)
!   arista-bl-lf-1  Border leaf  Mgmt 192.168.122.26  ASN 65103  VTEP 10.255.1.13
!   arista-bl-lf-2  Border leaf  Mgmt 192.168.122.27  ASN 65104  VTEP 10.255.1.14
!   arista-lf-1     Leaf         Mgmt 192.168.122.28  ASN 65101  VTEP 10.255.1.11
!   arista-lf-2     Leaf         Mgmt 192.168.122.29  ASN 65102  VTEP 10.255.1.12
!   (values from inventory/hosts.yml, host_vars/<switch>.yml and spine_asn in group_vars)
!
! Check after applying:  show banner motd    (then log in again to see it)
! Remove:                no banner motd
